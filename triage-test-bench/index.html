Triage Engine Test Bench

A simple demo of what a triage engine evaluation tool should enable: comparing triage tools side by side to inform the choice of tool.

Each clinical vignette is told to every engine by three LLM simulated personas, and every answer is scored against the clinical expectation.

8 clinical vignettes×3 LLM simulated personas×3 engines→Scored against the clinical expectation
